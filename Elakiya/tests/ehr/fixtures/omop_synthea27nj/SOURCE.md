Excerpt of the Eunomia "Synthea27Nj" OMOP CDM 5.4 dataset (synthetic, Synthea-generated):
https://github.com/OHDSI/EunomiaDatasets/blob/main/datasets/Synthea27Nj/Synthea27Nj_5.4.zip
(Apache-2.0; downloaded 2026-09-16; zip md5 0c3f3b9176482a413bf6cdf64fe4536b).

Rows copied unchanged for up to 12 persons (statin users, persons with the demo conditions, and the
first persons with lipid panels): PERSON, CONDITION_OCCURRENCE (all rows), MEASUREMENT (lipid panel rows
plus 20 other rows), DRUG_EXPOSURE (statin rows plus 15 other rows), and the CONCEPT rows referenced by
those tables. CONCEPT_ANCESTOR is empty in the source dataset and is not included.
